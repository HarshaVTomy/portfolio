About

A short introduction to who I am and what I work on.

Jr. Data Analyst — forecasting, NLP & analytics

I'm an MCA graduate with a mathematics background, currently working as a Jr. Data Analyst at Caspar Technologies in Kochi. My day-to-day runs from automated data collection through to the dashboard a client actually reads — Python and n8n workflows pulling from the YouTube, Twitter, Facebook and Google Drive APIs, feature work and modelling, then Power BI reporting on top.

On the forecasting side I build SKU-level sales pipelines using statistical, machine learning and deep learning models — Prophet, Chronos and PatchTST — combined into ensembles and evaluated with MAPE and WMAPE. On the language side I've built NLP pipelines with the OpenAI API, Geopy and HDBSCAN, and run topic modelling and clustering with Sentence-BERT for political and brand intelligence.

I like problems where the analysis has to survive contact with a real decision, and I'm open to roles in data analysis, machine learning and analytics engineering.
